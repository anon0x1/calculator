How your loan is calculated
Quick reference — expand any row
01 Monthly EMI ⌄
Reducing Balance: EMI is calculated from the loan amount, annual interest rate and tenure. Interest reduces as the outstanding principal is repaid.
EMI = P × r × (1 + r)ⁿ ÷ ((1 + r)ⁿ − 1)
02 Interest & repayment ⌄
Reducing Balance: Total interest is the amount paid above the original principal.
Compound Interest: Interest compounds monthly on the original principal and the repayment is spread across the selected tenure.
03 Additional charges ⌄
Processing Fee, GST and Other Charges are added to the loan repayment to show the total cost.
Total Cost = Principal + Interest + Additional Charges
